## Where the referral code field appears

The referral code field on Binance appears during the signup process, not after the account is created. When you open the Binance referral link, the code RMCTNB5R fills in automatically. If you navigate to the standard signup page directly, you will see an empty field labeled "Referral ID" or "Referral Code" that you can fill in manually. The field sits below the email or phone number input on the registration form. The most reliable approach is to always start from the referral link, as this guarantees the code is attached before the account exists.

## Why entering the code after signup does not work

Binance ties the referral benefit to the moment of account creation. The 20% fee discount is not a coupon you apply later. It is a flag set on the account at registration. If you sign up without entering RMCTNB5R, the account is recorded as organic, not referred. Customer support cannot convert an existing account into a referred one after the fact. This timing requirement is the single most common reason people miss out on the discount. They create an account first, then look for where to enter the code. By that point, the window has closed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I enter the referral code after I already have a Binance account?

No. The referral code must be entered during registration. Binance does not allow retroactive application of referral benefits to existing accounts.

### Does the 20% fee discount apply to futures trading as well?

The referral discount applies to spot trading fees. Futures fee structures are governed by separate terms and may not carry the same discount.

### How do I know the code RMCTNB5R was applied to my account?

Check the fee page in your account settings. A 20% discount indicator should appear next to your trading fee tier. If it does not appear, contact Binance support with your registration details.
