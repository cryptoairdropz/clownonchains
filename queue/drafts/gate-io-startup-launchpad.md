Gate.io Startup is the exchange's launchpad for new token sales, offering users early access to projects before they list on the open market. The process is not a guaranteed discount — allocations are limited, terms vary by project, and the tokens you receive can drop sharply after listing.

## How Gate.io Startup works

Gate.io Startup operates as a token sale platform where new projects sell tokens to exchange users before public listing. Users commit a portion of their exchange balance in exchange for an allocation of the new token. The sale typically runs in phases — a subscription period followed by a distribution period — and the token lists on Gate.io spot trading after distribution.

The exchange has operated since 2013, and its Startup platform has hosted numerous project launches over the years. The platform's large user base gives projects access to a broad pool of potential buyers, which is part of the value Gate.io offers to launching teams. For users, the appeal is simple: buy before the crowd.

## How allocations are determined

Gate.io Startup allocations are not guaranteed. The platform uses a tiered system based on your average GT (Gate Token) balance over a holding period. Users who hold more GT receive higher allocation tiers, meaning a larger share of the sale. Without holding GT, you may receive a smaller allocation or none at all.

This structure means the launchpad is primarily designed for users who are already committed to the Gate.io ecosystem. Casual users who arrive only for specific sales may find their allocations limited.

- Holding requirement: average GT balance during the snapshot period determines your tier.
- Subscription cap: each tier has a maximum commit amount per user.
- Distribution ratio: if total subscriptions exceed the sale size, allocations are scaled down proportionally.

## What you receive and when

After the sale concludes and the token lists, you receive your allocated tokens in your Gate.io spot wallet. The listing price may be higher or lower than the sale price. In practice, many launchpad tokens experience significant price movement in their first hours of trading — in both directions. Some tokens double or triple before collapsing; others never recover their sale price.

You can typically trade the token immediately after listing on Gate.io spot. Withdrawal availability may be delayed depending on the project's technical setup. Some tokens have no lock-up and can be sold right away; others have vesting schedules that delay your ability to sell for days or weeks. Always check the specific sale terms before committing funds.

## Risks of participating in Startup sales

- Price decline: launchpad tokens frequently drop below their sale price after listing. Early access does not guarantee profit.
- Lock-up periods: some Startup tokens have vesting schedules that prevent immediate selling.
- Project failure: new tokens carry extreme risk. A significant share of launchpad projects fail entirely.
- Opportunity cost: capital committed to a sale is locked until distribution and cannot be used for other trades.
- GT price exposure: if you buy GT to qualify for a higher tier, you are exposed to GT's own price volatility.

Treat Startup participation as high-risk speculation, not a discount on future gains. The same token you buy at the sale price may trade at half that price within hours of listing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to hold GT to participate in Gate.io Startup?

GT holding improves your allocation tier. You can participate without holding GT, but your allocation will be at the lowest tier. Higher GT balances during the snapshot period unlock larger allocations.

### Can I sell a Startup token immediately after listing?

It depends on the project's terms. Some tokens have no lock-up and can be traded right away. Others have vesting schedules that delay your ability to sell. Check the specific sale terms before committing funds.

### Does the Gate.io referral code MONADSSS help with Startup allocations?

No. The code MONADSSS provides a fee discount and signup bonuses but does not affect your Startup allocation tier. That is determined solely by your GT holdings during the snapshot period.
