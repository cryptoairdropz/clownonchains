Axiom is built to minimize the time between your click and the transaction landing on-chain. This matters most for new tokens, where the difference of a few seconds can mean a significantly better entry price. ## What goes wrong when you trade

The most common issue is slippage on thin tokens. If a new token has limited liquidity, your order can move the price significantly before it fills. The second common problem is failed transactions due to network congestion — you pay gas even when the trade does not go through. Always check the slippage setting before confirming a trade on a new or low-liquidity token.

## Risks of using a trading bot

Funds sit with the bot rather than your own wallet, which means you are trusting Axiom's infrastructure with your capital. Solana tokens are extremely volatile, and even a fast execution tool cannot protect you from a token that collapses after you buy. The 1 percent fee compounds quickly if you trade frequently, which can erode your returns even when individual trades are profitable.

Check the wallet's full trading history, including losing periods. Look for consistency rather than spectacular single trades. A wallet that has been steadily profitable over six months is more reliable than one that had one lucky trade last week. This requires setting your parameters in advance: the amount to spend, the maximum price you are willing to pay, and the slippage tolerance.
