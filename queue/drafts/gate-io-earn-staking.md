## The lock-up trade-off

Fixed-term earn products offer higher APY but restrict access to your funds. If the market moves sharply during the lock-up period, you cannot sell or transfer the staked assets. Flexible products offer lower rates but let you withdraw at any time.

The choice between flexible and fixed-term comes down to your outlook. If you expect the token to rise significantly, locking it up in earn means missing that upside. If you expect it to fall, the earn yield may not offset the depreciation. There is no universally correct answer — it depends on your market view and your need for liquidity. Consider splitting your allocation between flexible and fixed-term products to balance access and yield. This way, part of your position earns a higher rate while the rest remains available if the market moves.

## Risks of exchange-based earn products

- Custodial risk: your earn funds are held by Gate.io, not in your own wallet. The exchange's solvency and security directly affect your principal.
- Rate reduction: platforms can lower earn rates with little notice, reducing your expected returns.
- Lock-up risk: fixed-term products prevent you from acting on market movements.
- Token-specific risk: the underlying token's price volatility can dwarf the earn yield. A 20% APY means little if the token drops 50%.
- Regulatory risk: earn products may be classified differently across jurisdictions, affecting their availability and tax treatment.
