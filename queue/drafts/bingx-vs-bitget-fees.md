BingX and Bitget look similar on the surface — both emphasize copy trading, both list hundreds of altcoins, both offer referral fee discounts. But the fee structures behind the marketing differ in ways that matter depending on how you trade.

## The headline fee comparison

On spot trading, both BingX and Bitget charge a standard taker fee of 0.10% and a maker fee of 0.10%. At first glance, they are identical. The difference emerges when you look at what each platform charges for other product types and how their referral discounts apply. Both exchanges offer a 20% fee discount through their referral programs. BingX applies this through the code 6XOZMMGL; Bitget has its own referral mechanism. The discount brings the effective spot taker fee down to roughly 0.08% on both platforms for referred users.

## Where the structures diverge

The platforms differ most clearly in their approach to new token listings and copy trading protections. Bitget lists 900+ pairs and has built its reputation on listing new tokens quickly, sometimes ahead of Binance. Its copy trading product includes fund protection — a feature that shields copied positions under certain conditions. BingX lists 800+ pairs and focuses on making copy trading accessible to beginners.

Its interface is simpler, and the minimum deposit is around $10, which is lower than most major exchanges. BingX does not offer the same fund protection mechanism on copy trading positions that Bitget provides. For futures trading, both platforms offer competitive rates, but the exact fee tiers vary by trading volume. High-volume traders should check each platform's current fee schedule rather than relying on headline numbers.

## Referral and bonus comparison

BingX offers up to $1,000 in signup bonuses plus the 20% fee discount through its referral program. Bitget offers up to $5,500 in staged rewards plus a similar fee discount. The Bitget bonus figure is larger, but like BingX's, it unlocks in stages tied to deposit and trading volume milestones. The BingX code 6XOZMMGL gives new accounts immediate access to the 20% fee discount and the bonus program. Both platforms require KYC verification before trading, and both release bonuses gradually rather than as a lump sum.

## Which platform costs less overall

For pure spot trading at low volume, the cost difference between BingX and Bitget is negligible once referral discounts are applied. Both charge the same base rate and offer the same percentage discount. The total cost depends more on what you trade and how often. If you trade new tokens immediately after listing, Bitget's faster listing process may give you access to pairs that are not yet on BingX. If you trade primarily small-cap pairs, both platforms carry the risk of wide slippage on illiquid orders, which can cost more than the fee difference between them.

## Risks to compare

Both platforms carry risks that are structural to their business models.
- Small-cap liquidity: both exchanges list tokens with thin order books, where slippage can exceed your fee savings.
- Copy trading risk: following another trader's strategy does not guarantee profit, and past performance does not predict future results.
- Regulatory status: neither platform is licensed in every jurisdiction. Check your local rules before depositing.
- Bonus conditions: the large headline bonus figures on both platforms come with volume requirements that may force you to trade more than you intended. Compare the current fee schedules on both platforms before deciding, as rates can change.
