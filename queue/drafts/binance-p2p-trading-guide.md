Binance P2P lets users buy and sell cryptocurrency directly with other individuals using local currency, without going through the exchange's order book. It supports hundreds of payment methods including bank transfers, mobile wallets, and cash deposits. The platform uses escrow to protect both sides. This guide explains how P2P works, how to trade safely, and what to watch out for.

## How Binance P2P trading works

On Binance P2P, buyers and sellers post advertisements stating the price, payment method, and trade limits. When a buyer initiates a trade, Binance locks the seller's crypto in escrow. The buyer sends fiat payment directly to the seller using the agreed method. Once the seller confirms receipt, Binance releases the crypto to the buyer. This escrow mechanism is what makes P2P safe. Neither party can run off with the other's funds because the exchange holds the crypto until payment is confirmed.

## Supported payment methods and regions

Binance P2P supports a wide range of payment methods depending on the user's region. Common options include bank transfer (SEPA, SWIFT, and local networks), mobile money services like M-Pesa in Africa, e-wallets such as Skrill and Neteller, cash deposits at partner locations, and gift card trades in some markets. The availability of each method depends on the seller's preferences and the buyer's verified region. Binance verifies the identity of all P2P users through KYC, which reduces but does not eliminate fraud risk.

## Safety rules every P2P trader should follow

- Only trade with sellers who have a high completion rate and substantial trade history
- Never communicate outside the Binance chat system; it removes your protection
- Always verify payment in your account before confirming release of crypto
- Do not accept trades where the payment name does not match the seller's verified name
- Start with small amounts until you trust a counterparty
- Report any suspicious behaviour to Binance support immediately

The most common P2P scam involves a buyer who sends a fake payment confirmation screenshot. The seller, seeing what looks like a successful transfer, releases crypto from escrow. Hours later, the bank reverses the payment. To avoid this, always log into your actual banking app to verify the funds arrived before confirming the trade.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Binance P2P safe?

Yes, when used correctly. The escrow system protects funds during the trade. Most losses occur when users move communication outside the platform or fail to verify payments.

### What payment methods are available?

Available methods depend on your region and the seller's settings. Bank transfer is the most common, but mobile money, e-wallets, and other options are supported in many countries.

### Can I cancel a P2P trade after initiating?

You can cancel before payment is made. After you mark the order as paid, cancellation requires the seller's agreement or support intervention.
