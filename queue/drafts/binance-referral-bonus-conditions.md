## Actions that void referral benefits

- Creating multiple accounts from the same device or IP address to claim the bonus repeatedly
- Using the referral code on an account that already existed before the code was applied
- Engaging in wash trading or other manipulative practices to meet volume thresholds
- Violating Binance's terms of service in any way
- Using the account primarily for arbitrage between Binance and other platforms

Binance monitors for abuse systematically. Accounts flagged for referral fraud have all associated bonuses revoked and may be suspended. To maximise the referral bonus legally, complete KYC as soon as you register, trade consistently rather than in a single large burst, and read the specific terms attached to each voucher in the rewards centre. Binance runs two separate programs: the referral program for regular users and the affiliate program for high-volume promoters. The referral code RMCTNB5R belongs to the standard referral program, which offers the 20% discount and the $100 bonus cap. The affiliate program offers higher commissions but requires application and approval. Confusing the two programs is a common source of misunderstanding.
