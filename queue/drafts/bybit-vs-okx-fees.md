Bybit and OKX are two of the largest crypto exchanges in the world, and both offer competitive fee structures. But they are not identical. This article compares their fees side by side so you can decide which platform charges less for your trading style.

## Spot trading fees

Bybit charges a maker fee of 0.02% and a taker fee of 0.055% on spot trading. OKX charges a maker fee of 0.08% and a taker fee of 0.10%. For spot traders, Bybit is significantly cheaper on both sides of the order book. If you frequently place limit orders that add liquidity, Bybit's maker fee is less than a quarter of OKX's. For takers who cross the spread, Bybit's 0.055% is roughly half of OKX's 0.10%. Over hundreds of trades, this difference compounds into meaningful savings, especially for high-volume traders.
