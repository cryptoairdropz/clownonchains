## What Dawn is and why people use it

Dawn is a Telegram-based trading bot that lets you trade tokens across multiple chains without leaving the chat interface. ## Making your first trade

Once your wallet is funded, you can buy a token by pasting its contract address into the chat. Dawn will pull up the token's details and let you set your buy amount. The bot executes the trade within seconds and shows you your position and PnL in the same chat window.

For selling, select the open position from your portfolio list and choose the sell percentage. Dawn processes the sale and returns the proceeds to your bot wallet.

Start with a small amount for your first trade. On-chain execution involves variables — slippage, network congestion, gas — that are easier to understand with a position size you are comfortable losing.

## Risks and what can go wrong

A Telegram bot is not your wallet. Your funds sit with the bot, not in a wallet where you hold the seed phrase. If the bot's infrastructure is compromised, your balance is at risk. Keep your bot wallet balance limited to an amount you are prepared to lose entirely.

Other risks specific to on-chain trading include:

- Rug pulls on new tokens, where developers drain liquidity and the price goes to zero
- Slippage on low-liquidity tokens, where your executed price differs significantly from the quoted price
- Failed transactions that still cost gas fees
- Phishing bots that impersonate legitimate trading bots — always verify you are in the official chat

Dawn is a tool, not a guarantee. The same features that make it fast — direct on-chain execution, minimal confirmation steps — also mean mistakes are expensive. A wrong contract address, a misconfigured slippage setting, or a token with locked liquidity can all result in permanent loss of funds.

Start with a small test trade before committing any meaningful amount. Learn how the interface feels, how long execution takes, and how the bot displays your positions. Once you are comfortable with the mechanics, you can increase your position sizes gradually.
