With a $10 deposit, you can trade most spot pairs on BingX. The exchange lists over 800 trading pairs, including major cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum as well as smaller altcoins. For futures trading, the minimum position size depends on the pair and leverage used.

## Risks of starting small

Starting with a small deposit is a sensible way to learn, but it does not eliminate risk. Crypto is volatile, and even a $10 position can lose most of its value quickly if you trade a micro-cap token with thin liquidity. Small-cap pairs on BingX have wide slippage, which means you may get a worse price than expected when entering or exiting a position.

Without KYC, account recovery is harder if you lose access to your email or device. Enable two-factor authentication from the start and store your recovery codes securely. The funds you deposit are always at risk — only deposit what you are prepared to lose entirely.
