CopyFomo is a Telegram trading bot built specifically for new-token copy trading and trending-token signals. This review examines its features, fee structure, and the specific risks that come with its focus on the highest-risk category in crypto.

## What CopyFomo does

CopyFomo is designed to catch new tokens faster than manual trading allows. It combines two core features: built-in copy trading that mirrors other wallets, and trending-token notifications that alert you when a token is gaining momentum.

The bot runs entirely in Telegram, which means no separate application to install and no browser needed. You set up a wallet, deposit funds, and start copy trading or following trending signals from the chat interface.

CopyFomo supports multichain trading, so you are not limited to a single network. The exact chains supported are not specified in the bot's documentation, but the multichain label means you can trade tokens across different blockchains from the same interface.

## The new-token problem

CopyFomo's focus on new tokens is both its selling point and its biggest risk. New tokens are the highest-risk category in crypto. A large share of new tokens end in a rug pull, where developers drain liquidity and the price collapses to zero.

When you copy trade a wallet that specialises in new tokens, you inherit that risk directly. The wallet you are following might have a good run of picking winners, but one bad entry can wipe out months of gains. New tokens are volatile by nature — they can double in minutes or go to zero just as fast.

Trending-token notifications carry the same risk. A token that is trending is often already past its initial pump. By the time you receive the notification and execute a buy, early entrants may already be selling into your order.

## Custody and operational risks

Like all Telegram bots, CopyFomo holds your funds in the bot's wallet rather than giving you a seed phrase you control. If the bot is compromised, your deposited assets are at risk. There is no insurance fund and no proof-of-reserves publication.

Additional risks include:

- Slippage on thin tokens, where your executed price differs significantly from the quoted price
- Failed transactions that still cost network fees
- Phishing bots that impersonate CopyFomo — always verify you are in the official chat
- The wallet you are copying can change strategy at any time without notice

## Who CopyFomo suits

CopyFomo suits traders who want a Telegram-native tool for new-token copy trading and are comfortable with the extreme risk that comes with that category. If you understand that most new tokens fail and you are only risking money you can afford to lose entirely, the bot provides a fast, convenient interface.

It does not suit risk-averse traders, anyone who cannot afford to lose their full deposit, or traders who want a diversified toolset beyond new-token speculation.
