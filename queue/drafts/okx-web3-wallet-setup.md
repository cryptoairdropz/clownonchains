## What the OKX Web3 wallet is

The OKX Web3 wallet is a self-custody wallet built by the OKX exchange. Unlike the exchange's custodial account, the Web3 wallet gives you full control of your private keys. You can trade on DEXes, provide liquidity, stake tokens, and interact with DeFi protocols across dozens of blockchains including Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, and BSC. The wallet is non-custodial, meaning OKX does not hold your funds. You are solely responsible for your seed phrase. If you lose it, there is no customer support that can recover your assets. This is the trade-off for self-custody: full control comes with full responsibility.

## How to set up the wallet

To set up the OKX Web3 wallet, open the OKX app and navigate to the Web3 wallet section. You can create a new wallet or import an existing one using a seed phrase. If you create a new wallet, the app generates a 12-word seed phrase that you must write down and store securely.

- Open the OKX app and go to the Web3 wallet section
- Choose to create a new wallet or import an existing one
- Write down the 12-word seed phrase on paper
- Confirm the seed phrase by selecting the words in order
- Set a wallet password for additional security

## Backup and security basics

Your seed phrase is the only way to recover your wallet. If you lose it, your funds are gone forever. Write it down on paper and store it in a secure location. Never store it digitally — no screenshots, no cloud storage, no email.

- Never share your seed phrase with anyone
- Store it on paper in a secure, private location
- Consider a metal backup for fire and water resistance
- Enable biometric authentication on the app
- Whitelist withdrawal addresses for additional security

## Risks and what goes wrong

Self-custody wallets eliminate counterparty risk but introduce user error risk. The most common mistakes are losing seed phrases, falling for phishing attacks, and approving malicious smart contracts. Once a transaction is signed and broadcast, it cannot be reversed. OKX's watch list warns that Web3 features are more complex than a standard exchange. The referral code ENJOYDISCOUNT reduces your trading fees, but no code can protect you from user error. Take the time to understand self-custody before you deposit significant funds.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the OKX Web3 wallet safe?

The wallet itself is secure because it is self-custody — you hold the keys. But you are fully responsible for your seed phrase. If you lose it or share it, your funds can be stolen with no recourse. The security of your funds depends on your own practices.

### Which chains does the OKX Web3 wallet support?

The wallet supports dozens of blockchains including Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, and BSC. You can trade, bridge, and interact with DeFi protocols across all supported chains from the same app. The list of supported chains is regularly updated.

### What is the difference between OKX and OKX Web3?

OKX is a custodial exchange where the platform holds your funds. OKX Web3 is a self-custody wallet where you hold your own keys. They are separate products with different risk profiles. You can use both side by side, transferring funds between them as needed.
