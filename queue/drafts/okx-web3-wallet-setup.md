## What the OKX Web3 wallet is

The OKX Web3 wallet is a self-custody wallet built by the OKX exchange. Unlike the exchange's custodial account, the Web3 wallet gives you full control of your private keys. You can trade on DEXes, provide liquidity, stake tokens, and interact with DeFi protocols across dozens of blockchains including Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, and BSC. The wallet supports a wide range of decentralised applications and protocols. The wallet is non-custodial, meaning OKX does not hold your funds. You are solely responsible for your seed phrase. If you lose it, there is no customer support that can recover your assets. This is the trade-off for self-custody: full control comes with full responsibility. The OKX Web3 wallet is designed to make self-custody as user-friendly as possible while maintaining the security benefits of a non-custodial wallet.

## Risks and what goes wrong

Self-custody wallets eliminate counterparty risk but introduce user error risk. The most common mistakes are losing seed phrases, falling for phishing attacks, and approving malicious smart contracts. Once a transaction is signed and broadcast, it cannot be reversed. This is why education and caution are essential when using a Web3 wallet.

- Phishing sites can trick you into entering your seed phrase
- Malicious smart contracts can drain your wallet when you approve them
- Sending funds to the wrong address or chain can result in permanent loss
- DEX smart contract risk still applies even with a secure wallet
- Social engineering attacks can trick you into revealing your seed phrase
- Fake support agents may ask for your seed phrase — never share it

OKX's watch list warns that Web3 features are more complex than a standard exchange. The referral code ENJOYDISCOUNT reduces your trading fees, but no code can protect you from user error. Take the time to understand self-custody before you deposit significant funds. The learning curve is steep, but the security benefits are worth the effort.

### Is the OKX Web3 wallet safe?

The wallet itself is secure because it is self-custody — you hold the keys. But you are fully responsible for your seed phrase. If you lose it or share it, your funds can be stolen with no recourse. The security of your funds depends on your own practices.

### Which chains does the OKX Web3 wallet support?

The wallet supports dozens of blockchains including Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, and BSC. You can trade, bridge, and interact with DeFi protocols across all supported chains from the same app. The list of supported chains is regularly updated.

### What is the difference between OKX and OKX Web3?

OKX is a custodial exchange where the platform holds your funds. OKX Web3 is a self-custody wallet where you hold your own keys. They are separate products with different risk profiles. You can use both side by side, transferring funds between them as needed.
