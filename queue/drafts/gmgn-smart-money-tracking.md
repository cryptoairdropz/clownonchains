## What smart-money tracking means

## How to use the data

Smart-money tracking is not a buy signal. It is a research tool. The data tells you what profitable wallets are doing, not what you will profit from. Here is how to use it responsibly:

- Look for wallets with a long track record, not just one good trade. A wallet that bought one token early and got lucky is not smart money. A wallet that has been profitable across dozens of trades over months is.
- Check what the wallet is holding now, not what it bought last week. By the time you see the data, the wallet may have already sold.
- Cross-reference with other data. Smart-money holdings are one input among many. Look at token holder distribution, liquidity, and trading volume before making a decision.
- Size your positions based on your risk tolerance, not based on the wallet's size. A smart-money wallet may be trading with ten times your capital.

## What the data does not tell you

Smart-money tracking has important limitations:

- Past profitability does not guarantee future performance. A wallet that was profitable last quarter may be losing money this quarter.
- You do not know the wallet's full portfolio. GMGN shows on-chain holdings, but the wallet may have positions on other chains or in protocols that are not visible.
- You do not know the wallet's timing. The data shows what the wallet bought, but not when it plans to sell.
- Smart-money wallets can be wrong. They can buy tokens that go to zero, just like any other trader.

The data is a starting point for research, not a trading signal to follow blindly.

## Risks of following smart money

Following smart-money wallets carries specific risks:

- You are always behind the wallet you follow. By the time you see their purchase and execute your own, the price has moved.
- Slippage on thin tokens can erode any advantage you gained from the smart-money signal.
- Smart-money wallets can change strategy at any time without notice.
- Solana tokens are extremely volatile. Even a well-timed entry can result in significant losses if the token dumps.
