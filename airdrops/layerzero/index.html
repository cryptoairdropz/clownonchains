Common questions

Is the LayerZero airdrop still active?

LayerZero is currently at "Snapshot checking" with an allocation of TBA. Deadlines and snapshots change quickly, so always check the project's official channels before spending gas.

How do I qualify for the LayerZero airdrop?

Complete the tasks listed above using a single wallet, and keep activity consistent over time. LayerZero runs on Multichain. Projects weight genuine, sustained usage far above one-off transactions.

Does the LayerZero airdrop cost anything?

The tokens are free, but on-chain tasks cost gas. Budget a monthly cap, prefer cheap periods, and remember that most farms pay nothing — treat each interaction as a small bet, not an investment.
