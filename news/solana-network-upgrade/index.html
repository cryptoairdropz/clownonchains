Solana network upgrade promises reduced latency for bots

Market Intel · 5 hours ago · Not financial advice

The Solana network has rolled out a significant upgrade aimed at reducing transaction latency, a move that directly benefits automated trading bots and high-frequency traders. The upgrade optimizes block propagation and validator communication, resulting in faster confirmation times. Developers report noticeable improvements in bot execution speed and reduced slippage. This enhancement strengthens Solana's position as a leading chain for decentralized trading applications.
