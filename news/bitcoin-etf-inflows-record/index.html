Bitcoin ETF inflows surge to record highs this week

Market Intel · 2 hours ago · Not financial advice

Spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds saw unprecedented inflows this week, with institutional demand pushing total assets under management to new heights. Analysts point to growing confidence among traditional investors as a key driver. The surge comes amid broader market optimism and increasing adoption of Bitcoin as a legitimate asset class. Experts suggest this trend could continue as more financial institutions gain exposure to cryptocurrency markets.
